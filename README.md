
# Police in City

Police in City is a 3D police simulation game developed in Unity with C#. Players take on the role of a police officer, patrolling the city, monitoring traffic violations, and conducting inspections of vehicles and citizens.

## Gameplay Features

### Traffic Patrol & Vehicle Inspections

- Monitor vehicle speeds and identify traffic violations.
- Patrol city roads and interact with vehicles.
- Perform vehicle searches and inspections.

[Watch Traffic Patrol & Vehicle Inspection Gameplay](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9e303ff7-9940-406b-a538-6641a2e88a54)

### Citizen Inspections

- Interact with citizens during police patrols.
- Perform searches and inspection procedures.
- Experience interactive police duty mechanics.

## Technical Highlights

- 3D gameplay systems developed with Unity and C#.
- Interactive vehicle and character inspection mechanics.

- Player interactions within a 3D city environment.
